Simple setup

From products to published in minutes

1

Select your products

Pick individual products or an entire collection from your Shopify store. Images, descriptions, and pricing are pulled in automatically.

2

Choose a template

Pick from editorial layouts built to make your products look stunning. Set your catalogue title and season label. No design skills needed.

3

Go live instantly

Click Generate and your catalogue is live on your store with a shareable URL. Embed anywhere with a single App Block in your theme editor.

Why Catalogify

The storefront experience your brand deserves

⚡

Built from your products automatically

No uploading. No copy-pasting. Catalogify pulls images, descriptions, and pricing directly from Shopify and lays them out beautifully.
